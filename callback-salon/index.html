The pilot, honestly

How the pilot works — including the part most pages hide

Texting can’t legally run on a free, anonymous number — so here is exactly how we handle it

Real SMS sending requires a carrier-registered number in your salon’s name (US 10DLC rules), through a provider such as Twilio. That registration typically takes 2–3 weeks for carrier approval, and the carrier’s own usage charges — typically under $15–$30/mo, paid directly to the carrier — are yours, at cost. We don’t mark them up, and we won’t pretend they’re zero.

So the pilot starts as a concierge setup: we write your message with you, connect your booking link, and get your registration filed in week one. While approval is pending, we show you exactly what every missed caller will receive. You pay the $39 only when your text-back is live — if registration stalls, you pay nothing.

Questions

Before you email us

Do I need to change my salon’s phone number?

No. Your number stays yours. The text-back is registered properly in your business’s name — that registration is exactly the 2–3 week step described above, and it’s why your texts won’t get flagged as spam.

Does it work with my booking system?

Almost certainly yes. The text carries a link, so any system with an online booking page works: Square Appointments, Vagaro, Booksy, Fresha, GlossGenius, Acuity, or a page of your own. If you don’t have online booking yet, we’ll help you turn on a free one during setup.

What does the $39 cover, and what doesn’t it?

It covers the service: the missed-call detection, your custom message, reply routing, and pilot support. It doesn’t cover the carrier’s own texting charges (typically under $15–$30/mo, paid by you directly to the carrier at cost) — no honest provider can make those disappear, because the messages legally have to come from your registered number.

Will clients think a robot is texting them?

The message says it’s an automatic reply from your salon and offers a human reply path (“reply and we’ll help”). In practice, clients mostly tap the booking link — that’s the point. You approve the exact wording before launch, and you can change it anytime.

Is there a contract or a setup fee?

No contract and no setup or “launch” fee — unlike the $349 launch fee on Aplos Front Desk. Month to month, cancel by email.

Why salons only? What about my friend’s dental office?

Salons are where we can do this properly today: the owner decides, booking is the whole job, and there’s no health-data regulation on a haircut appointment. Dental needs HIPAA/BAA-covered texting, so it comes later, with the right provider — we’d rather say that plainly than sell you the wrong thing.
