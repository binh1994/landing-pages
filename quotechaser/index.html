There are 111,207 plumbing, heating & A/C establishments and 83,342 electrical establishments in the US (Census County Business Patterns 2023) — and 76.8% of HVAC firms have fewer than 10 employees, with nobody whose job is chasing quotes. QuoteChaser is that follow-up, done for you. Email-first on purpose: no SMS API, no carrier approval wait.

    

      
DAY 1
“Just checking it landed”
Confirms they received the quote and can open it. Catches wrong-email and spam-folder losses early.

      
DAY 3
Answer the hidden objection

DAY 7
Gentle deadline
Notes that scheduling and parts pricing are held until a date. Honest urgency, no pressure tricks.

      
DAY 14
Clean close-out
“Should I close this out?” — the email that gets the most replies, because saying no feels safe.

Honest answers

    
The caveats are on the page, not in the fine print.

    
Is this email or text?
Email-first at v1 — that’s how it stays $99 with no launch fee and no 2–3 week carrier approval wait. If you want SMS later, you connect your own Twilio number; carrier usage typically runs under $15–$30/mo, paid by you directly to the carrier.

    
Do I have to switch off Jobber / ServiceTitan / QuickBooks?
No. QuoteChaser is a standalone chaser for open quotes. Shops already on a full field-service suite with follow-up built in may not need it — it’s built for the long tail that isn’t on one.

    
Will customers find the emails annoying?
Four short emails over 14 days, each with an easy “no thanks” path, and the sequence stops the moment they reply. The Day 14 close-out exists specifically so nobody gets chased forever.

    
What if it doesn’t recover a job for me?
Cancel anytime — no contract, no launch fee to lose. Judge it on the Day 14 review: emails sent, replies, and bookings are all on your board.

    
Who is this for?
HVAC, plumbing, and electrical shops — especially 1–10-person firms in Texas and beyond — that send quotes by email, PDF, or paper and rarely follow up more than once.

    
How does the free trial on this page work?
Create a trial account above and you get 3 days with the full in-browser app. The account and your trial data are stored only in this browser (localStorage) — there is no server account, and trial follow-ups are drafts only. When the 3 days end, the app locks; subscribing via PayPal is how you continue with real follow-up emails sent for you.
