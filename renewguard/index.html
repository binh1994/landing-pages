01

Document vault

One record for every license, COI, workers’ comp policy, permit, OSHA card and W-9 — company documents and per-tech documents side by side, each with its issuing authority, state, holder and expiry date. Upload the PDF or a photo of the card; confirm the expiry date in one click.

Reminder schedule at 90 / 60 / 30 / 14 / 7 days

Every expiry gets the full ladder of reminder dates, for you and for the named tech whose document it is — starting early enough to book a CE course or chase an insurance agent, not the week the license dies. FAQ

Questions a shop owner actually asks

Why $24 when DueLantern and RenewCue charge $49?

Because the product is deliberately narrower: your own licenses, crew certs and insurance for a 1–10-person shop, one state’s CE rules done properly at launch — no vendor marketplace, no 50-state directory, no per-vendor pricing. The running cost is a database, file storage and scheduled reminders (about $50–$120/mo total at 100 customers, per the research estimates above), so $24/mo is a sustainable price, not a teaser. Sources: duelantern.com/for/contractors · renewcue.app

Is LicenseRoadmap’s $229/mo real — and can I buy it?

The price was published on their site when checked on 14 Nov 2026 — Essentials $229/mo for 8 techs, Pro $429, Business $799 — but the product was waitlist-only, so a small shop could not actually buy it that day. It is useful as proof the top of this market tolerates far more than $24 — not as a plan you can switch to today. Source: licenseroadmap.com

Can I actually pay $24/mo on this page today?

There is no card form on this page itself — PayPal handles the payment. The 3-day free trial runs right on this page, in your browser — start it in the trial section above — and you are never charged for the trial period.

Which licenses and CE rules are covered at launch?

Texas, TDLR-licensed electrical, HVAC and plumbing trades — including CE-hour tracking per tech. Florida (DBPR) is the planned second state. One open question from the research, stated plainly: whether the Texas TDLR public register exposes licence expiry dates in bulk (vs. name/address only) was not verified — RenewGuard does not depend on it, because your dates come from your own documents at setup, but any claim about pre-built prospect lists should be treated as unconfirmed.

Are those fine amounts ($15,000 / $25,000 / $165,514) the law?

They are the figures published on competitor pages — DueLantern’s contractor page and LicenseRoadmap’s site, read 14 Nov 2026 — not quotations from the California statutes or OSHA. Penalties vary by state, trade and violation, and change over time. Treat them as the vendors’ published ranges and check your own state’s current schedule before relying on a number. Sources: duelantern.com/for/contractors · licenseroadmap.com

What happens at the end of the 3-day trial?

Nothing automatic — there is no card on file to charge; the trial never bills you. When the 3 days end, the in-browser tracker locks to read-only: your licences stay visible, editing stops, and subscribing through PayPal is how you continue. If the tracker earned its keep (usually the first time a GC proof pack goes out in one click), you move to $24/mo month-to-month, paid through PayPal. If it didn’t, you keep your exported documents and walk away; the vault export is yours either way.
