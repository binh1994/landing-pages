Straight answers

How does the 3-day free trial work?

Create a trial account on this page (name, email, password) — it is stored in this browser only, and your 3 days start at that moment. During the trial you can use the script builder and storyboard preview and submit trial video requests: 1 per day, 3 in total, each for a 20-second video. Trial videos are reviewed manually — approved requests are delivered within 48h. When the 3 days end, or all 3 requests are used, the app locks and your work stays visible, read-only, until you order through PayPal.

Is any video generated automatically during the trial?

No. The trial is text only: sample, script, storyboard preview, and a request you copy or email to us. No video is made — and no token is spent — until a human reviews and approves your request.

Will the person in my ad look like me in every clip?

In Format B we build every clip fresh from your one photo and check the face at the start, middle, and end of each clip. Small drift can still happen — that is the honest state of AI video today. A clip that drifts is remade fresh; if it still doesn’t pass, you don’t pay for that video. If you can’t accept any risk, choose Format A: no face, no problem.

How fast is delivery, really?

48 hours from script approval for a standard 30-second ad. A remake for a failed clip can add a day — we’d rather be a day late than send you a face that isn’t yours.

Can you do Vietnamese voiceover?

Yes. Vietnamese-owned salons, rental businesses, and shops in the US are one of our first audiences — English or Vietnamese voice and captions, same $99.

Why not just use Runway or Veo myself for $12–20/month?

You can — the tools are cheap and their prices are in the table above. What you’re buying here is the labour and the checking: script, consistent presenter, second-by-second QA, remakes, captions, and a finished file. If you enjoy making videos, the tools win. If you’d rather run your shop, we win.

Do you make real-estate or mortgage videos?

Yes, as done-for-you jobs at the same $99/video — a listing video from your photos, or a monthly presenter update. We don’t sell those as separate subscriptions, and for listing photos we follow disclosure rules for digitally altered images (e.g. California AB 723 from 1 Jan 2026) — ask us and we’ll flag what applies in your state.
