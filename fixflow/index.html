“Something is broken” is not a work order.

Requests land across texts, email, calls and portals. Then comes the chase: what is broken, where, how bad, how urgent, can the vendor get in? Five or six messages later you finally know — and if the vendor still shows up without the model number or a photo, you've paid $75–$150 for a visit that fixed nothing.

One link in, one clear job out.

No app for tenants to download. No app for vendors either. FixFlow's whole job is turning a vague complaint into a complete, provable work order.

STEP 1

Tenant scans & reports

A QR code in the unit (or a web link) opens a short structured form: what's broken, which fixture, photos, urgency, and permission to enter. Two minutes, no account.

STEP 2

Complete before it reaches you

The form won't submit half-empty. If the fixture, photos or access window are missing, the tenant is asked right there — not by you, over six texts. Complete requests skip straight to “ready to dispatch.”

STEP 3

Vendor gets one SMS

Assign the job and your handyman or plumber receives a single text with the address, fixture, photos and access window — everything needed to fix it on the first visit.

STEP 4

Photo proof, kept forever

The vendor replies with after-photos by text. You get a permanent record per unit: request, photos, status, and estimated vs actual cost — ready for deposits, disputes and tax time.

Questions landlords ask

Do my tenants need to download an app?
No. Each property gets a QR code (and a plain web link). The tenant scans, fills the structured form with photos, and is done — no account, no download. Vendors work the same way: everything runs over SMS and web links.
Does FixFlow collect rent or do accounting?
No — deliberately. Rent, screening and accounting are what the free and paid suites already do well. FixFlow covers the part they treat as a side feature: getting a repair from “something is broken” to fixed, with proof. Many landlords run FixFlow alongside a free suite for rent.

Send a trial request below and you'll get a workspace for your properties with the full feature set for 3 days. If you continue, it's $9/mo flat, month-to-month. If you don't, nothing is charged and your records export with you.

Sirkonia — the closest equivalent, with QR intake, SMS dispatch and photo proof — charges $19/mo for the first 2 units and $26/mo at 3 units (sirkonia.com, checked 14 Nov 2026; it was still pre-launch at that check). FixFlow prices flat at $9/mo for the whole 5–50 unit portfolio instead of scaling with unit count. One avoided diagnostic visit ($75–$150) pays for roughly 8–17 months of FixFlow.
