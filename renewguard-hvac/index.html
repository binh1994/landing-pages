Straight answers

Does RenewGuard file my renewal for me?

No. RenewGuard watches the dates and emails you at 90, 60, 30, 14, 7 and 0 days out. You still renew with TDLR yourself — the product exists so the date never sneaks up on you.

Where does the license data come from?

From you, or from TDLR's own public register — the State of Texas publishes the full license file free, with no API key, updated daily, via TDLR License Search files and data.texas.gov. One honest caveat from that register: TDLR publishes no phone number and no street address for A/C contractors, so if we ever contact you about this page, it will be by email or through your Google Business listing — never a cold call from the list.

I'm a plumber, not A/C. Does this work for me?

Not yet — and we'd rather say so. Texas plumbers are licensed by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, a separate register from TDLR that isn't connected yet. A/C contractors are covered today; plumbing is an open item, listed plainly rather than promised.

Why $39 when the electrical version is $24?

Because an HVAC shop carries more than one license: crew certifications and OSHA cards ride on the same account. Both pages sit at roughly half the named competitor's price — CertifyTrack Pro's $79/mo is the anchor here.
