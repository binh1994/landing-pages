FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Is $37 really the whole price?

Yes — $37/mo flat, all locations, no setup fee, no contract. It's priced at about half of NiceJob Starter ($75/mo, checked Oct–Nov 2026). If we ever change pricing, existing customers keep their rate for 12 months.

Do you send the review requests automatically?

Not in v1 — and that's deliberate honesty, not a missing checkbox. Automatic sending needs Google API approval and a registered messaging carrier, which costs real money per message. In v1 you send from your own email/phone with our templates (about 30 seconds), and ReviewTracker tracks everything and drafts every reply. Owners who send personally also tend to get warmer reviews.

What about bad reviews?

They're where reply drafts matter most. ReviewTracker drafts a calm, specific, non-defensive reply that future customers — who read your response more than the complaint — will judge you by. You always approve before anything is posted; nothing is ever posted automatically.

I have two locations. Do I pay twice?

No. $37 covers all your locations. GatherUp charges $99/mo for one location; per-location pricing is exactly the model we built this against.

There's a $19 tool. Why pay $37?

Fair question. Tradeloper (~$19/mo) sends email review requests, and if that's all you need, buy it. ReviewTracker adds the two things that turn requests into a system: an ask-by-ask tracker so nobody slips through, and reply drafts in your voice for every review — including the bad ones.
